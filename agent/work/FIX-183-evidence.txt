both under one lock; `received` adds to the FIFO when the bound is in service and otherwise starts the READ; a READ in service
runs on its own `rail8-peer-serve` virtual thread whose finally counts `open` down and hands its place to the head of the FIFO,
on a fresh thread - the same path for a return and for a throw. Nothing of `serve`, no thread start and no wait under the lock.

thread serving the next head.
