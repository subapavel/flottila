# Packet FIX-123 (class A) — the persist warning's sentence is unasserted

Module: `flottila-core`, test-only. Row: `agent/BACKLOG.md`, FIX-123 (dep FIX-121 `done`).

## The defect

`Trail.persist()` logs at WARNING (`Trail.java:176-179`):

    "message " + p.key().messageId() + ": the body " + p.key()
      + " could not be made durable (" + persisted.cause()
      + "); its rows are recorded metadata-only"

FIX-54 pinned the `message <id>: ` prefix and FIX-121 fixed how the two cases filter the captured
records, but no case asserts the sentence itself. The reviewer's hand mutant for FIX-121 changed
"could not be made durable" to "could not be saved" and all 13 `TrailTest` cases stayed green
(`agent/work/FIX-121-mutB.log`). The class javadoc (`Trail.java:32-33`) promises the operator this
wording tells them the rows are metadata-only; nothing holds that promise.

## Acceptance

1. A hand mutant on the sentence at `Trail.java:176-179` fails at least one case. Both halves are
   load-bearing for the operator and both are asserted: the cause phrase
   ("could not be made durable") and the consequence tail ("its rows are recorded metadata-only").
2. The FIX-54 prefix assertions (`TrailTest:388`, `:409`) and the key assertions keep passing,
   untouched. This row adds assertions; it rewrites none.
3. The filtering stays as FIX-121 left it — each case selects its own records by its own key, never
   by a bare id substring. `"m-w"` is a prefix of `"m-wait"`; that trap has now been hit three times
   (FIX-54, FIX-121 twice). Do not reintroduce it.
4. The cause's own text (`persisted.cause()`, e.g. "pg down") is **not** asserted as part of the
   sentence: it is the store's words, not Trail's, and pinning it would make this test fail when an
   unrelated fake changes its exception message. Assert only Trail's own wording around it.
