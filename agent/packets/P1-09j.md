# P1-09j — Durable DLQ bodies (F-7, D-062). Designer draft 2026-10-27, approved by the architect.

## Acceptance
- `Parking.park(e, step, failure)` persists `e.body().key()` in `e.body().store()` and only after that completes calls `dlq.park`. In sequence, never `Future.all`.
- A persist that fails or throws (Pg down, IAE, `BodyTooLargeException`) fails the park with that cause; `dlq.park` is never called.
- In `wrap`, the step's own failure surfaces with the persist failure suppressed (existing path, not a ParkedException).
- The worker leaves such an item to its lease (LEAVE + WARNING). Past maxAttempts the existing exhausted park parks the ingress body at step 1, which Delivery already made durable.
- Sync flows never park and never persist (D-045).
- `TieredBlobStore.Stored` becomes a `private static final class` with the same five accessors (identity equality), so a same-size rewrite during a persist keeps the rewrite.
- `Node.java` refusal (≈:151): `"Postgres synchronous_commit is " + value + " for the node's sessions; it must be on, remote_write or remote_apply (D-061): off acknowledges a commit before its WAL flush, local does not wait for a synchronous standby"`; the `@throws` (≈:95): `a {@code synchronous_commit} other than on, remote_write or remote_apply ({@code off} does not wait for the WAL flush, {@code local} for a synchronous standby)`.
- Signatures unchanged: Parked, Dlq, PgDlq, InMemoryDlq, FlowBuilder, FlowWorker, Replay. No api, schema or migration change.
