# FIX-265 - the last of replay leaves the api, the restart statement and `/retry` (class B)

Row: `grep -n "^| FIX-265 " agent/BACKLOG.md`. Decision D-156 (the owner, 2026-11-15: no replay), finding F-55.
FIX-264 took `Replay` out of the core; nothing writes the prop `replayOf` any more. This row removes the constant, the
two guards that read it and the words that name it. Expensive to reverse (a public constant of `rail8-api` goes):
`draft — review at hand-over`. Line numbers below are as of FIX-264's commit; trust the symbol, not the number.

## What is true today (read before writing)

- `Envelope.REPLAY_OF` (`platform/api/.../Envelope.java`:38-39) has no writer in any `src/main`. Its readers are two
  guards: the restart statement's `and props->'replayOf' is null` (`runtime/queue/PgWorkQueue.java`:305, in `RESTART`)
  and `AdminServer.retry`'s twin (`runtime/admin/AdminServer.java`:710, with the words ", a replay" at :712).
- A replay's DLQ row never carried the worker's prop `attempt` (`Envelope.ATTEMPT`'s Javadoc, :41-44: "dropped by a
  replay"), so the attempt check beside each guard already refuses such a row. The in-memory queue has no DLQ and
  never had the guard (`WorkQueue.java`:276-282, the contract's last sentence).
- So removing the guards changes one thing only: a DLQ row at step 1 that carries the worker's attempt k AND some
  other prop is restarted whatever that prop is called. Rows written before this commit keep the prop as inert data;
  no migration touches them.
