## The reading (FIX-168's, unverified for this case — the red confirms it or you hand back)

A first `Journal.open` forces `segments/` twice on the opening thread (the directory's creation, segment
1) and starts the pre-open of segment 2 on a thread of its own (`journal-open-2`), which ends with its
own force of `segments/`. The case waits until three forces of `segments/` have returned before it arms
its holds; the opener's two and the witness persist's one meet that wait without the pre-open's. Its
holds take the first force of `segments/` by any thread, so a late pre-open's force can be held and
failed as the persist's — the fault FIX-168 removed from `JournalDirForceOrderTest` (commit 5365f393).
