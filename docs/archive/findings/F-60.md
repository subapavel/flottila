# F-60 — two reads of the polling contract are one instant

Date: 2026-11-16 · Task: FIX-240 · ARCHITECTURE section: §11 (version 3.33)

- **Assumption in the document:** §11: the component contract suite is what "every real and fake
  component must pass", "ACK only after durable write" among its properties. For a polling
  consumer the suite says: a file stays while the handler's answer is pending, also while the
  consumer is stopping (`PollingConsumerContract.java`:468), and a stopped consumer delivers
  nothing (:496). A reader takes a green suite as proof of both.
- **How it fails in practice:** most of the suite does prove the first. A removal before the
  answer, at any timing, is caught by its missing redelivery wherever the answer that follows
  keeps the file: the pending case (:255, then a retryable Nack and the file required again at
  :258-259), and the cases at :281, :303, :335, :495 and :546. Two reads are one instant:
  - :468, the stop case. It reads the directory once, after it saw the handler's call and
    `stop()` handed back its future; the answer that follows is an Accepted (:469-472), so the
    file goes anyway and nothing later can tell an early removal from a right one. A component
    that removes early only while it is stopping, and later than that read, passes.
  - :496. It reads the stopped consumer's count once, after two rounds of a running consumer. A
    stopped consumer that delivers later than that read passes.
  The self-test's own broken variant showed the first: removing the file right after it had
  started the handler's thread, it failed the suite in every run through the other cases, but
  passed the stop case in 1 run of 20 under load on the branch (FIX-240's row) and in 1 run of 3
  on 2026-11-16. A read cannot prove "never"; the suite can only make the read late, and every
  wait before it is paid by every green run of every component.
- **Severity:** minor — two checks are narrower than their sentences; no mechanism changes. No
  shipped component was read for either fault by this row.
- **Evidence:** `agent/work/FIX-280-before-run2.log`:16-32 (the stop case missing from the
  failures of the broken variant while the pending case is among them, 2026-11-16); FIX-240's
  row (1 of 20 under load; commit c42887a's rows); the contract's lines, read by the architect
  and, in review, by the reviewer.
- **Change made:** none here. FIX-240 orders the self-test's variant so that its fault is always
  before the stop case's read; the contract's reads are as they were. CONSIDER-30 weighs what
  the two reads could do: a later or second read with its cost per green run measured, or a
  sentence in the contract's Javadoc that says what they cannot see.
