# F-54 — the imported mapping engine's service is built on other rules than this document's

Date: 2026-11-15 · Task: BRAND-3 · ARCHITECTURE section: §12 (version 3.25)

- **Assumption in the document:** §12 lists `mapping/` as an area that "moves in with its own parent
  pom" and says only that no platform module depends on it at first. Nothing says whose rules bind
  it. The document's rules — Vert.x 5.1, one virtual thread per message, a socket only through the
  Component SPI, no message refused for its size, the licence list — are written as the
  repository's.
- **How it fails in practice:** the engine arrived with its history (D-150) and with a service that
  follows its own ADRs: Quarkus 3.40 on Vert.x 4.5 (the mapping's ADR-0014), its own HTTP listener on
  127.0.0.1, request bodies read whole and refused above 10 MB (ADR-0024), worker threads and a
  platform thread for a test run (ADR-0014, ADR-0020), no authentication in its milestone 1, a wider
  licence list with exceptions per artifact (ADR-0012, ADR-0014, ADR-0017), no jqwik (ADR-0018). The
  engine's JPMS modules depend on none of the service's frameworks (ADR-0014, ADR-0016). Read as the
  repository's, this document's rules are broken by code that passes its own 17 822 tests and that
  no one asked to rewrite; a session that reads them so "fixes" one area by the other's rules.
- **Severity:** major — the document's rules were written as the repository's, and §12 now says
  where they hold: a normative paragraph, not a clarification. Nothing the platform builds or ships
  changes.
- **Evidence:** the mapping's ADRs, POM and rule file as read on 2026-11-15
  (`agent/work/BRAND-3-scout-2.md` blocks A, C, E); `mapping/pom.xml:207-222` and
  `mapping/licence-filters.txt` for the exceptions per artifact; the mapping's suite as imported,
  17 822 tests, 0 failures (`agent/work/BRAND-3-s3-verify-counts.txt`).
- **Change made:** §12 states the scope (D-153). This document's design rules bind the whole
  repository except `mapping/`. `mapping/` is bound by its own ADRs and by `mapping/CLAUDE.md` until
  its embedding is designed; the embedding (a step over the engine's facade, what of the engine's
  dependency closure may enter the platform's class path, sizes, who may edit and deploy a mapping)
  needs a finding of its own and the owner's brainstorm first (MAP-1). Until then nothing outside
  `mapping/` depends on, packages or starts anything built from it (MAP-8 makes that a failing
  check), nothing built from it is published, and its licence list is frozen as imported. The
  root's test rules, working rules and context hygiene bind both areas (D-153 names the lines of
  the test rules that are the platform's own). Nothing in the mapping is rewritten to the
  platform's rules by this finding, and nothing in the platform is loosened.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md edit:** changelog 3.26 — §12: the area is in the repository; a new paragraph
  "Whose rules"; the `mapping/` row of the table.
- **PROGRESS entry:** BRAND-3 (step 3) marked `Deviation: F-54`.
- **Not decided here:** whether the mapping's service is ever shipped with a node or stays a
  development tool; whether the platform's `control` area (planned, Quarkus) and the mapping's
  service become one; whether the engine's modules alone should come under the root's licence list
  now; whether the root licence rule covers GPL-2.0 with the Classpath Exception. ISSUE-9 carries
  the licence lines and the scope to the owner.
- **For the owner's review:** the scope paragraph of §12 is `draft — review at hand-over` (ISSUE-9
  item 3). It adds no code and removes none, so reversing it is cheap today; it stops being cheap
  with every feature built under the mapping's rules, which is why D-153 holds the mapping to
  closing, fixing and observing until MAP-1. Rejected: rewriting the service to the platform's
  rules now (not asked); taking `mapping` out of the root reactor (it gives up the one checkout and
  one wrapper D-150 chose); a rule-by-rule table in §12 (it drifts).
