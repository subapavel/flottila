# F-104 — #118's jump builds the callee's frame at the take, not at the jump

- **Assumption**: ADR-0047 #118 "The jump" says the jump "builds the callee's frame, which holds the
  value and closure arguments". Decision bullet 1 lists the pending-jump fields as the callee, the
  input, the value arguments and the closure arguments.
- **How it differs**: on impl/118 (a3a0856), `JumpOp` records itself, the jumping activation's frame
  and the input. The taker builds the frame with `jump.frame(frame, in)`, and only if
  `jumpFrame() == frame` (CallOp around lines 64, 102 and 161). On the trampoline, a left-side call
  that is at most one sees its caller's jump first, so a jump must be taken only by the loop running
  in its own frame.
- **Also**: the implementer's premise "at most one means value form", used to call the check at line
  64 dead, is wrong. A left call that may break out is push form (grilling-adviser J3), with a
  witness filter that reference jq gives as `[5]`.
- **Severity**: minor. The frames are identical: `Argument` is the input, a cell or a constant. The
  cost is one reference field per `Active` and one checkcast per jump.
- **Evidence**: the #118 implementer's hand-back; the grilling-adviser's J1–J8.
- **Change made**: ADR-0047 #118 gains "Where a jump is taken". A J4 test kills the line-64 mutant.
  The eval path's javadoc gives J5's reason.
