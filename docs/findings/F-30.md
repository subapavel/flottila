# F-30 — a timed poll is how work is found today; the hand-off and its lost hand-on (P2-16, D-102 → D-121)

Date 2026-11-10. Status: resolved by P2-16a (hand-off, per-key lock) and P2-16b (post-commit work events, PT30S reconciliation);
P2-16c (NOTIFY inside the commit, its cost at CORE-GATE) open, blocked on the machine.
Assumed (ARCHITECTURE §4, D-102 direction): work is picked up by events and the node that saved a message runs it.
Practice: every saved message costs a claim round trip (accept poll), another node learns of work up to 1 s late, and every idle
flow claims once a second. Latent defect: a keyed completion (CLAIM_NEXT_OF_KEY, PgWorkQueue.java:104) and a same-key save on another
group can each miss the other's uncommitted row; the save then waits for a tick. The 1 s tick and the accept poll hide it; with a
30 s safety net it would show. Fix: P2-16a's per-key advisory lock taken by keyed inserts and keyed completes
(test aKeyedCompletionAndASameKeySaveNeverStrandTheSave).
Change: P2-16a writes the item leased to its node in the intake transaction when its worker has a reserved slot and the item heads
its key; P2-16b adds post-commit debounced LISTEN/NOTIFY events and a PT30S reconciliation claim; P2-16c measures NOTIFY inside the
commit at CORE-GATE. D-102 (2) "sent in the enqueueing transaction" is amended by D-121 until P2-16c.
Not live until CORE-5: events for spilled rows and expired peers' rows (a node claims only its own body_node rows, D-105); P2-16b's
live sources are the keyed completion whose next row is another node's, the operator's release and an intake item copying another node's ref.
