# F-14 — The durable ACK < 5 ms p99 is not reachable with two commits on a 4 vCPU cloud VM

Date: 2026-10-28 · Task: P1-GATE (measured in P1-10d) · ARCHITECTURE section: §7 Sizing, §13 Phase 1 (version 3.4)

- **Assumption in the document:** §7 Sizing and the §13 Phase 1 gate: "at-least-once durable ACK < 5 ms p99 native"
  (the prototype measured 7.4 ms p50 across the WSL2 boundary; "unverified co-located"). GATE.md check 9 carried it as a
  target.
- **How it fails in practice:** the ingress persists the body and the batched ingress record in parallel, then commits the
  work-item enqueue (Delivery.java:257-272) — two `synchronous_commit` fsyncs in sequence before the 202. On the 4 vCPU
  cloud VM with default Postgres 16 and client, node, partner and database on one box, p99 is 8.82 ms at c=1, 27.4 ms at
  c=8, 135 ms at c=64 (p50 4.24 / 11.9 / 60.9 ms). A partner that times out on a 5 ms budget would retry; none does in the
  documented use, but the promise in §7 was not true.
- **Severity:** major
- **Evidence:** bench/RESULTS.md "Phase 1 gate (P1-10)" durable ACK table (3 runs per cell, e129c77); P0-10 batcher pair
  p99 ≈ 8 ms at c=8 and 13 ms at c=64; issues/ISSUE-2.md; gate review of 350f71d (NOT_MET on this threshold alone).
- **Change made:** none in code. The owner answered ISSUE-2 with option 1+2 (2026-10-28): the Phase 1 gate records the
  durable ACK as measured with the machine named; < 5 ms p99 becomes the tuning target of Phase 2 row P2-08 (one commit
  for record + enqueue, group commit / `commit_delay`, re-measure on fast storage). The durable guarantee is unchanged.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md edit:** §7 Sizing sentence and §13 Phase 1 gate sentence restated; changelog row (3.4). GATE.md
  check 9 restated.
- **PROGRESS entry:** P1-GATE marked `Deviation: F-14`
- **For the owner's review:** decided by the owner (ISSUE-2, option 1+2). Option 3 (at-most-once for latency-critical
  producers) was considered and rejected as an answer, because it changes the guarantee rather than the latency.
