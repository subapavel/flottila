# F-8 — a replay from step n ≥ 2 reads node-local snapshot bodies

Date: 2026-10-27 · Task: P1-09c · ARCHITECTURE section: §6 (version 3.4)

- **Assumption in the document:** every recorded step can be replayed later ("monitoring is the product"; §6 replay from step n).
- **How it fails in practice:** `Replay.from` at step n ≥ 2 reads the snapshot body of step n−1, which only Delivery's ingress (step 1) and a park (P1-09j) persist. After a restart, or on another node, the body lives in the old node's memory or spool tier and the replay fails with IAE.
- **Severity:** major
- **Evidence:** P1-09j designer draft 2026-10-27 (open point 4).
- **Change made:** none yet. P1-09c maps the missing body to a 409 naming the step and that only the ingress replay survives a restart; durable snapshot bodies come with S3 (P2-05) and retention (P2-07).
- **ARCHITECTURE.md edit:** with P1-09c if the 409 becomes the documented behaviour
- **PROGRESS entry:** P1-09c marked `Deviation: F-8` if the documented replay changes
- **For the owner's review:** whether snapshot bodies of at-least-once flows should be persisted per step before S3 (Postgres cost) or replay past step 1 stays node-local until P2-05.
