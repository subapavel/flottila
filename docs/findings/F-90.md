# F-90 — the inline cap's backstop in `spend()` cannot be reached

- **Assumption** (F-85, F-86): every mutant of a class-C module is either killed or recorded. None is
  excluded.
- **How it fails**:
  - Since #114, an expansion is inlined only when its expansion measure fits.
  - The measure bounds what the lowering spends, so `++inlined > INLINE_CAP` is never true.
  - The mutant that removes the check or its throw survives.
  - The boundary and negation mutants are killed by `capCallsOfAOneNodeDefinitionCompile`.
- **Severity**: minor.
- **Evidence**: the #114 design; the grilling-adviser's ruling Q2 of 2026-11-15.
- **Change made**:
  - The backstop stays. If the measure is ever wrong, it still bounds the compile of an untrusted
    filter.
  - The surviving mutant is recorded here. No PIT exclusion is added.
  - ExpansionMeasureTest's property holds the measure to the bound.
