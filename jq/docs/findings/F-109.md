# F-109 — compile overflows below 512 KiB

- **Assumption**: DEVIATIONS "Parse depth": 512 levels fit a 512 KiB thread "with about a third to
  spare".
- **How it fails**: compiling `plain-descent` (IrDepthTest's `nested(30_001)`, PLAIN calls at the
  512-level IR bound) throws a raw StackOverflowError out of `compile`, from the Resolver's
  `array`/`built`/`lower` cycles under Parser frames. It throws on a 256 KiB thread in all four
  probes (`-Xint` and C1, both modes), and under C1 on a 384 KiB thread; `-Xint` passes at 384 KiB.
  The only catch of a stack overflow is at run time (`Executable.java:125`).
- **Severity**: minor. No document promises compiling below 512 KiB, but the spare is under a quarter,
  not a third, and an overflow in `compile` is not a clean error (F-42 rated a compiler crash major).
- **Evidence**: #199's C3 check 1, `.logs/199-c3-plain-descent.log` and
  `-larger.log` in the impl/199 worktree (b828a1e); the grilling-adviser's reading, 2026-11-16.
- **Change made**: the four cells are recorded as `compile STACK` (ADR-0046 #199, owner's ruling).
  Open, for a later round: a compile floor (bisect `-Xss` for `nested(30_001)` and `nested(513)`),
  and whether `compile` gives a clean error on a thread too small for it.
- **Widened (owner, 2026-11-16, #199 run A).** The Parser alone overflows too, 495–522 frames deep,
  on filters of about 100 brackets (W, hold, collect-descent: `D_f` 101–116), far below the parse
  bound of 512 levels or 127 parentheses (`Parser.java:105`), and only once the Parser is
  C1-compiled: a cold compile of W CONSERVATIVE fits 256 KiB, the same compile after 30 warm-up runs
  does not (`.logs/199-runA-c1-256-control.log` in the impl/199 worktree, fd2cd74). The calibration
  now compiles on a helper thread (ADR-0046 #199, run A stop), so the record no longer shows this;
  this finding carries it. Severity stays minor pending the compile floor with a C1-compiled
  Parser: bisect `-Xss` for the 127-parenthesis filter and `nested(30_001)`, cold and after 60
  compiles (adviser's estimate: about 74 brackets fit 256 KiB, about 197 fit 512 KiB). Open: a
  compile-floor ticket with a clean error from `compile`, and whether DEVIATIONS "Parse depth"
  needs a JIT qualifier.
