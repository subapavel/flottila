  commit check ("A's old `U*` equal #113's record").
- **How it fails**: seeded `edgeIsExact` on impl/199 b828a1e (after #114, #117 and #118) gives 6 of
  the 26 record `U*cold`; OPTIMISED T, M and E are flat (#118's tail calls) and 17 fall, by 20 to
  6 692 units (OPTIMISED recurse 12 684 → 5 992). Before the #118 merge, only OPTIMISED recurse and

  assert one direction only (K7), so no gate saw it.

  moved edge is 4 710 against `U_f` 1237 − D_f, and the `b`-setting shapes (W, collect-descent) did not

- **Evidence**: `.logs/199-c3-edge-compare.log` and `-pre118.log` in the impl/199 worktree; the
  grilling-adviser's attribution, 2026-11-16 (direction expected, magnitude unproven).
