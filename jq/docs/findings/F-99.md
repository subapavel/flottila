# F-99 — a value-form eval drops extra results silently, and a property failure hangs the fork

- **Assumption**:
  - ADR-0007 says a missed fact gives "never a wrong value".
  - ADR-0047 #116 lets a node run in the value form only where cardinality is at most one.
  - A regression fails fast, with a diagnosis.
- **How it fails**:
  - Where an `eval` runs over a push part that gives several results (Pipe, Bind, If, Foreach,
    Binds), it keeps the first and drops the rest without a check. A wrong cardinality fact would
    lose a result silently instead of failing.
  - `UnitWeightsPropertyTest` asserts `allSatisfy` over every probe. Under the hand mutants M2 and
    M7 of #116, AssertJ spent more than 30 minutes formatting the failure message, and the fork was
    killed (exit 143). In CI this becomes a bare 30-minute timeout with no diagnosis.
- **Severity**: major for the silent drop (a defensive gap against ADR-0007), minor for the hang.
- **Evidence**: the #116 Standards review (impl/116 at 6cc9970) and the implementer's mutant logs
  (`.logs/116-mut-M2/M7`).
- **Change made**:
  - The value-form `eval` of Pipe, Bind, If, Foreach, Binds and Comma throws
    `IllegalStateException` when a part the facts promise gives no result gives one. The check is
    always on. ValueFormTest builds each op over a wrong fact and expects the throw.
  - `UnitWeightsPropertyTest` checks the deepest probe's frame count, not every probe, and checks
    the run's outcome first. A failure names one probe and ends within seconds.
