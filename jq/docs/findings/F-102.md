# F-102 — two shapes under C1 set `b`; the rest lie between 24 and 100

- **Assumption**: one bytes-per-unit constant `b` fits every shape, near #111's provisional 96
  (ADR-0046).
- **How it fails**: the #113 matrix (impl/113 e6f4cc0, 234 cells) measures `b_cell` between 24 and
  100 for 11 of 13 shapes. Under C1, W gives 189 and collect-descent 167. With `b` = 192 and a
  280 KiB reserve, `U_f` at 512 KiB falls from 4096 − D_f to 1237 − D_f. That is about 30–60 levels
  of ordinary recursion before the trampoline, and about 11 for W.
- **Severity**: minor. Results are the same; deep recursion switches to the heap form earlier, so the
  cost is speed only, unmeasured (see below).
- **Evidence**: `.logs/113-calibration.tsv` in worktree agent-a0ba96dd95c2dc64f; the grilling-adviser's
  K2 and K4.
- **Change made**: none in #113 (K4: one `b`, as measured). Bytes per unit by node kind, or a larger
  512 KiB default, are the owner's later choice (owner-review). The trampoline's cost per level against
  the push form is unmeasured; a bench at depth 1000 with `stackBytes` 0 against 8 MiB would price it.
